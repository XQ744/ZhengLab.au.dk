Representative Work

Highly multiplexed imaging and deep visual proteomics.

The provided representative article introduces mipDVP and applies it to colorectal and tonsil cancer tissues to reveal spatially regulated tumor-immune interactions.

Molecular Cell · 2025

Deciphering functional tumor-immune crosstalk through highly multiplexed imaging and deep visual proteomics

Xiang Zheng, Andreas Mund, Matthias Mann · Molecular Cell 85, 1008–1023 · DOI: 10.1016/j.molcel.2024.12.023

  • Developed an advanced strategy combining multiplexed imaging, single-cell laser microdissection, and sensitive mass spectrometry.
  • Identified 21 cell populations and thousands of proteins while preserving spatial tissue context.
  • Revealed an immunosuppressive macrophage barrier that may impede T-cell infiltration in cold tumors.
  • Discovered spatial T-cell adaptations in hypoxic tumor regions and potential immunotherapy targets.
