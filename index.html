Research Focus

Understanding disease as a spatially organized ecosystem.

The lab combines clinical questions with high-dimensional imaging, laser microdissection, mass spectrometry, and computational interpretation to resolve molecular programs inside tissue.

01

Tumor-Immune Crosstalk

Decode how cancer cells, macrophages, T cells, and stromal neighborhoods interact to shape immune evasion and therapy response.

02

Spatial Proteomics

Build and apply mipDVP workflows that connect multiplexed tissue images with deep proteomic measurements at spatially defined cell populations.

03

Precision Medicine

Transform archived and clinical tissues into molecular maps that reveal biomarkers, vulnerabilities, and patient-specific therapeutic hypotheses.

Representative Work

Highly multiplexed imaging and deep visual proteomics.

The provided representative article introduces mipDVP and applies it to colorectal and tonsil cancer tissues to reveal spatially regulated tumor-immune interactions.

Molecular Cell · 2025

Deciphering functional tumor-immune crosstalk through highly multiplexed imaging and deep visual proteomics

Xiang Zheng, Andreas Mund, Matthias Mann · Molecular Cell 85, 1008–1023 · DOI: 10.1016/j.molcel.2024.12.023

  • Developed an advanced strategy combining multiplexed imaging, single-cell laser microdissection, and sensitive mass spectrometry.
  • Identified 21 cell populations and thousands of proteins while preserving spatial tissue context.
  • Revealed an immunosuppressive macrophage barrier that may impede T-cell infiltration in cold tumors.
  • Discovered spatial T-cell adaptations in hypoxic tumor regions and potential immunotherapy targets.

Platform

A tissue-to-proteome workflow built for clinical questions.

Spatial Selection

Proteomic Profiling

Measure thousands of proteins from selected cells using sensitive mass spectrometry.

Clinical Translation

Identify biomarkers, immune adaptations, and therapeutic targets for precision medicine.

Scientific Focus Areas

  • Spatial proteomics: developed mipDVP, now expanding to 3D and immune clone-resolved spatial multiomics.

  • Computational disease modeling: AI-driven integration of spatial omics data to predict disease progression and treatment response.
  • Precision medicine: translating spatial biomarkers into clinical diagnostics and therapeutic targets.
